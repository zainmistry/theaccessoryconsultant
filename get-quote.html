RFQ checklist — what to include

The clearer the brief, the faster we can respond accurately. Include as much of the following as you have:

  • Product type — e.g. earrings, rings, necklaces, bracelets, sets
  • Design files — CAD, sketches, tech packs, or clear reference photos
  • Materials — solid gold, gold filled, vermeil, plated, silver, stainless (and any preferences)
  • Stones — type, size, quantity if known (or “open to recommendation”)
  • Quantity — target order size (MOQ from 50 units)
  • Finish & details — plating preference, clasp/findings notes, branding/stamp needs if any
  • Engagement model — private label / OEM / custom / ODM
  • NDA — note if you need an NDA before sharing full designs
  • Timeline — your target launch or delivery window
  • Shipping country / delivery destination — e.g. United States (city/state or port preference if known); required so logistics can be scoped without assuming transit times
  • Company & contact — brand/company name, phone, email

You do not need every item to submit. Send what you have; we will ask for anything missing.

What happens after you submit

  1. We receive your RFQ and review the designs, materials, quantities, timeline, and shipping destination.
  2. We reply with questions if anything is unclear, or with feasibility notes and next steps toward CAD, sampling, and production.
  3. Once scope is aligned, we move into the standard path: CAD → sample → production.

We do not invent turnaround hours on the reply itself—response timing depends on the completeness of your brief and current queue. Typical manufacturing lead time after alignment is about 18 days CAD-to-delivery, subject to design and order specifics. International shipping to the US or other countries is confirmed separately once destination and method are agreed—we do not publish fixed transit SLAs here.
