CAD & sampling

Most programs follow a three-stage path:

  1. CAD — We develop or refine 3D designs so proportions, findings, and stone seats are production-ready.
  2. Sample — Physical samples let you check finish, fit, weight feel, and stone setting before you commit to volume.
  3. Production — Once samples are approved, we manufacture the ordered quantity to the agreed specs at our Mumbai factory.

Starting with CAD and sampling reduces risk on private-label and OEM runs. If you already have approved CAD or samples, we can often move faster into production after we verify feasibility.

NDA for private label & OEM

Confidentiality matters when you are launching a US line, protecting designs, or manufacturing under your brand. We offer NDAs for private-label and OEM engagements. Share your requirements at the quote stage and we will align on documentation before sharing sensitive design files.

Quality control

Quality control runs through sampling and production. Samples establish the approved standard for finish, construction, and stone setting. Production is checked against those approved samples and the agreed specifications. If something needs clarification before we start, we raise it during the quote and CAD review—not after goods leave the factory.
