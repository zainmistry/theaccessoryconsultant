Working at trend speed

Trend-led brands win by moving quickly and reordering what sells. Our process supports that:

  1. Brief fast. A sketch, a moodboard or a reference photo is enough to start. We develop original designs to your direction if you work with us as an ODM partner.
  2. CAD and render review. Quick iterations on shape and proportion.
  3. Sample. A physical sample shipped to you. Check finish and weight.
  4. Production. About 18 days from CAD to delivery for a typical program.
  5. Reorder. Bestsellers are reordered against the same approved sample so they match.

Building a fashion assortment

A balanced fashion assortment mixes a few reliable core styles with a rotating set of trend pieces. Core styles, such as a simple hoop or a chain, stay in your range all year and are reordered regularly. Trend pieces are small runs that test a colour, shape or motif for a season. With a minimum per design rather than per collection, you can keep the core stable while refreshing the trend layer every few weeks. Track sell-through by design so reorders go to the pieces customers actually buy, and retire slow sellers before they pile up in stock.

Original designs only

We manufacture your original designs or designs developed with you. We do not copy other brands' designs. Competitor references are welcome as direction, but the final design should be your own.

Finish and quality expectations

Fashion jewelry customers still expect pieces to look good after wearing. Talk to us about how each piece will be worn, and approve the finish on the physical sample before production. Production is checked against that sample. Specifications are confirmed per project in your quote.
