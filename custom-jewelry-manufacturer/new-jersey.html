Custom jewelry manufacturer for New Jersey brands

Northern New Jersey is the working back office of the New York jewelry trade. Wholesalers, importers and distributors operate from warehouses near Newark and the port, many brands keep fulfilment in New Jersey while selling in Manhattan, and the state's large South Asian community in towns like Edison and Iselin supports a busy Indian jewelry retail scene. For many NJ buyers, jewelry is a sourcing and logistics business first.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a New Jersey office. Our team serves New Jersey and NYC-metro clients remotely.

For wholesalers and importers

If you buy for resale to stores, you care about three things: repeatability, margin and documentation. We cannot quote prices here; those depend on your designs and come in the quote. What we can state:

  • MOQ from 50 units per design, letting you test new styles before committing to depth
  • About 18 days from CAD to delivery for a typical program
  • Production checked against an approved sample, so every reorder matches
  • NDAs offered for private label and OEM programs

International shipping to New Jersey is separate and confirmed once the method is known. Tell us about your importing arrangements in the brief.

Fulfilment-centre brands

Many D2C and Manhattan brands warehouse in New Jersey. We ship production directly to your NJ fulfilment partner. Give us your packing and labelling requirements in the brief so goods arrive ready for your 3PL.

Time zone

New Jersey is on Eastern time, nine and a half to ten and a half hours behind Mumbai. Most questions are answered overnight.

Reorder planning for store accounts

Wholesalers serving chains need to keep store accounts in stock without overbuying. Track sell-through by design, reorder bestsellers against the approved sample, and drop slow styles early. Since our minimum applies per design, you can rebalance an assortment between seasons by reordering only the winners. Tell us your forecast in the brief and we will plan production windows around it.
