Custom jewelry manufacturer for Portland brands

Portland's jewelry scene grew out of craft. Many Oregon brands started at a single bench, selling at markets on Alberta Street or online, and grew until the founder could no longer make every piece by hand. That moment, when demand outgrows the studio, is usually when Portland makers start looking for a production partner.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with its factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Portland office. Our team serves Oregon clients remotely.

Scaling a handmade line without losing its character

Makers worry that production will flatten what made their work special. Here is how we approach a handmade design:

  • Start from your piece. Send a finished original. We build CAD that captures its shape, texture and proportions.
  • Compare side by side. You receive a physical sample to put next to your original.
  • Approve or revise. Production only begins once the sample feels like yours.
  • Keep it small. MOQ starts from 50 units per design, so you can move one bestseller into production while keeping one-of-a-kind work at your bench.

Organic textures, hammered finishes and irregular forms can all be modelled, but some take more CAD iterations. Tell us which details matter most.

Talking honestly about sourcing

Portland customers ask hard questions about materials and origin. Our advice is to only make claims you can document. Pieces we manufacture are made in India. Material and stone options are confirmed per project, and if a specific sourcing claim matters to your marketing, raise it in your brief so we can discuss what can and cannot be documented. We do not make claims on this page about certifications.

Materials Oregon brands use

Sterling silver and gold filled are common for everyday pieces; solid gold for heirloom work; lab-grown diamonds and semi-precious stones for stones.

The practical bits

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Portland is separate and confirmed once the method is known. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs. Portland is on Pacific time, twelve and a half to thirteen and a half hours behind Mumbai, so feedback you send in the afternoon is usually answered by your next morning.

Who we suit and who we do not

We suit makers ready to produce at least 50 units of a design and who want consistency across those units. If every piece you sell is unique, keep making it yourself. Many Portland brands do both: production for core designs and the bench for limited pieces. Our low MOQ guide helps you decide which designs to move first.
