Custom jewelry manufacturer for Las Vegas brands

Every year the jewelry trade comes to Las Vegas. JCK Las Vegas and the shows that run alongside it gather retailers, designers, wholesalers and suppliers from across the world, and for many US buyers it is the week they decide who will make next year's lines. Outside of show week, Las Vegas has its own jewelry economy too: resort and casino boutiques, wedding chapels and bridal retailers, and a fast-growing base of local brands.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with its factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Las Vegas office. Our team serves Las Vegas clients, and buyers who meet suppliers in Las Vegas, remotely.

Before, during and after the show

Before show week

If you plan to launch or pitch a new line at a trade show, you need samples in hand. Brief us early with sketches, references or CAD. We build CAD, revise it with you and make physical samples you can bring to the show.

During show week

Use the show to gauge buyer demand for each style. Because our MOQ starts from 50 units per design, you can sample broadly and commit only to what buyers order.

After show week

Send us the final quantities. We produce against the approved samples and ship to your address. Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days for a program. International shipping to Las Vegas, or to wherever your stock is held, is separate and is confirmed once the method is known.

Buyers we work with in and around Las Vegas

Resort and casino retail. Boutiques inside resorts sell to visitors who want a memorable piece. Private label lines let a boutique offer designs no one else carries.

Wedding and bridal. Las Vegas hosts a very large number of weddings. Bridal retailers need rings and bands in solid gold with natural or lab-grown diamonds, made to CAD. See our engagement ring case study.

Trade-show buyers from elsewhere. Many people who search for a jewelry manufacturer in Las Vegas are not local at all. They are retailers and brands planning a show visit. If that is you, we are happy to start the conversation before the show so you arrive with samples and numbers.

Local D2C and fashion brands. Vegas founders selling online want the same thing as D2C founders everywhere: low minimums, consistent finish and quick reorders. Our low MOQ guide covers it.

Las Vegas FAQ

Will you be exhibiting at JCK?

We do not claim a show presence or a Las Vegas office. Our team serves clients remotely from Mumbai; contact us before your show visit and we can work on samples ahead of it.

Can I get samples in time for a trade show?

Share the show date in your brief. We plan CAD and sampling backward from it and will tell you what is realistic.

MOQ starts from 50 units per design.

About 18 days for a typical program. Shipping time to Las Vegas is separate.
