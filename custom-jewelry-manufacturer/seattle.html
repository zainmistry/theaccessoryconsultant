Custom jewelry manufacturer for Seattle brands

Seattle's jewelry scene grew out of makers. Many Pacific Northwest brands started at a bench in a home studio, sold at craft fairs and markets, and built a following online. The style leans minimal and nature-led: clean silver, small gold pieces, organic textures, stones that echo the coast and the mountains. Outdoor and lifestyle brands headquartered in the region also add jewelry to their ranges. The common moment we hear about is the point where a maker can no longer keep up by hand.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Seattle office. Our team serves Seattle and Pacific Northwest clients remotely.

From your bench to a production run

If you have been making every piece yourself, handing production to a factory raises fair worries. Will it look like my work? Will the texture survive? Will I lose control of quality? Here is how we address each one.

It starts with your piece. Send us your handmade original, or clear photos and measurements. We build CAD from it so the production version matches your design.

You approve the sample. A physical sample made in our factory ships to you in Seattle. Compare it side by side with your original. Nothing goes into production until you approve it.

Production is checked against that sample. Every piece is inspected against the approved reference.

Your designs stay yours. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs.

MOQ starts from 50 units per design, which is often a realistic first step for a maker moving from handmade batches to manufactured stock.

Timing and time zones

Seattle is on Pacific time, twelve and a half to thirteen and a half hours behind Mumbai. That gap means your day and ours barely overlap, and in practice it works in your favour: comments sent in the afternoon are often handled by the following morning. For a quick live conversation we schedule calls in your early morning or evening.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Seattle is separate and confirmed once the destination and method are known. If you are aiming for a holiday market, a craft fair or a seasonal launch, tell us the date.

Is a factory right for you yet?

Not every maker needs one. If you sell a few pieces a month and love making them, keep going by hand. A manufacturer makes sense when demand outpaces your bench, when you want wholesale accounts that need repeatable stock, or when you want to spend your time designing rather than producing. Our jewelry product development guide and RFQ guide help you prepare.
