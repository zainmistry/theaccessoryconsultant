Custom jewelry manufacturer for Atlanta brands

Atlanta mixes two jewelry worlds. On one side is wholesale: AmericasMart hosts major gift, home and apparel markets where boutique owners from across the Southeast buy their seasonal lines. On the other is culture: Atlanta's music, entertainment and creator economy has made statement jewelry part of personal brand, and many artists, stylists and creators now want their own lines. Add a strong community of Black-owned boutiques and D2C brands, and Atlanta is one of the most active jewelry markets in the South.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with its factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have an Atlanta office. Our team serves Atlanta clients remotely and ships production to your Georgia address.

Three kinds of Atlanta brief

"I need a line for market."

Boutique and wholesale brands showing at or buying for AmericasMart want a full range of samples, then production on the styles that sell. We develop CAD and samples first. MOQ starts from 50 units per design, so you can carry a wider line without committing to deep stock on every style.

"I am launching merch for my audience."

Artists, creators and stylists building their own jewelry brand care about recognisable design, quality that matches their image, and keeping the drop secret until launch. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs. Bold chains, pendants with a logo or initial, and statement rings are common starting points. Our custom jewelry manufacturer guide explains how custom design works.

"I run a D2C brand and need a better factory."

Established online brands moving manufacturing want consistency. Every production piece is checked against the sample you approved. Our D2C manufacturing guide covers what to ask a new supplier.

Remote, but not hands-off

You will never need to fly to Mumbai to work with us. Here is what remote production looks like for an Atlanta client:

  • Day one: you send a brief with photos, sketches or CAD, quantities and a delivery address.
  • NDA: signed for private label and OEM before files are shared.
  • CAD: our team models each piece and sends renders. You comment, we revise.
  • Sample: a physical sample ships to Atlanta. Approve it, or ask for changes.
  • Production: made and checked against the approved sample.
  • Delivery: packed and shipped to your Atlanta store, studio or warehouse.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Atlanta is separate and confirmed once the method is known. Atlanta is on Eastern time, about nine and a half to ten and a half hours behind Mumbai, so we usually reply to the day's questions overnight.

Materials for statement and everyday pieces

Statement chains and pendants often start in gold plated, gold vermeil or stainless steel; higher-end pieces move to solid gold with natural or lab-grown diamonds. Our men's jewelry case study is a useful reference for bolder designs. We will help you match the material to your price point, and you approve the finish on the physical sample.
