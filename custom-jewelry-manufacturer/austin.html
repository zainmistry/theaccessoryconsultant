Custom jewelry manufacturer for Austin brands

Austin has become one of the most productive places in America to start a consumer brand, and jewelry is no exception. The city has already produced nationally known jewelry companies, and behind them is a deep bench of founders, creative directors and boutique owners on South Congress and in East Austin who want to build the next one. Many of them come from tech or marketing and treat a jewelry launch like a product launch: test, measure, iterate.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have an Austin office. Our team serves Austin and Central Texas clients remotely.

Built for test-and-learn launches

If you think in experiments, our terms are designed to fit:

  • Small test batches. MOQ starts from 50 units per design. Launch five designs, see which sells, reorder the winners.
  • Fast iterations. Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days, so a reorder or a variant can follow data rather than guesswork.
  • Repeatable quality. Every reorder is made against the same approved sample.
  • Protected IP. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs.

International shipping to Austin is separate from the factory timeline and confirmed once the method is known.

Typical Austin product mix

Austin brands tend to sell a colourful, personality-driven look: statement earrings, charm necklaces, stackable rings and stones in bright colours. We often see:

  • Gold plated and gold vermeil for trend-led collections
  • Semi-precious stones and artificial stones for colour at accessible prices
  • Sterling silver for Western-influenced and everyday pieces
  • Lab-grown diamonds for founders moving into fine jewelry

Our fashion jewelry manufacturing page and D2C brands guide cover these categories.

Festival and event drops

Austin's festival calendar creates natural drop moments. If you are planning a limited collection for an event weekend, tell us the date and we will plan CAD, sample and production around it. Build in time for sample approval and international shipping.

How we work with founders who are new to jewelry

You do not need to know jewelry manufacturing jargon. Send moodboards, competitor examples you admire (we will not copy them, but they help us understand direction) or rough sketches. As an ODM partner we can develop original designs to that direction. Our OEM vs ODM guide explains the options.

Austin is on Central time, ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai. Send feedback before the end of your day and revisions usually arrive by morning.
