How remote production works from Chicago

Chicago is about ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai. In practice, most clients send comments at the end of their day and see updated CAD or answers the next morning.

Step one, quote. Send what you have: sketches, photos, a spec sheet or CAD. Include quantities per design, material and the delivery address. Our RFQ guide lists what to include.

Step two, NDA and CAD. For private label and OEM work we sign an NDA before design files are shared, then build 3D models for review.

Step three, sample. A physical sample ships to you in Chicago. Check it against your spec: weight, finish, clasp, stone setting.

Step four, production and QC. Pieces are made and checked against the approved sample.

Step five, shipping. Finished goods are packed and shipped to your Chicago warehouse, store or distribution centre.

Why not just source domestically?

Many Chicago buyers do both. Domestic workshops are useful for repairs and rush one-offs. A dedicated export factory is built for running the same design at volume to one standard, with a low starting minimum. If you are comparing, ask every supplier the same questions: MOQ, CAD-to-delivery time, how samples are approved and whether they sign an NDA.
