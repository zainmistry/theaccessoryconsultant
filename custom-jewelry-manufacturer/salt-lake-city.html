Custom jewelry manufacturer for Salt Lake City brands

Utah has a jewelry market unlike any other state. Couples tend to marry young, which makes bridal and wedding bands a large share of local retail. The Salt Lake and Utah County area is also known as a base for direct-sales and social-selling companies, many of which sell jewelry or accessories through networks of independent sellers. And the state's outdoor culture supports active and minimal lifestyle brands.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Utah office. Our team serves Salt Lake City clients remotely.

Bridal volume

Utah bridal retailers often sell a large number of similar engagement mountings and bands. A house-design program suits this: choose designs, approve samples, and stock in quantity. MOQ starts from 50 units per design.

Typical bridal materials are solid gold with natural or lab-grown diamonds. Our bridal jewelry manufacturer page and lab-grown diamond page explain these programs in more depth.

Direct-sales and social-selling companies

Companies that sell through independent consultants need jewelry that photographs well, ships easily, and can be reordered reliably as catalogues change. Their key requirements are usually:

  • New designs for each seasonal catalogue
  • Consistent production across reorders
  • Confidentiality before catalogue launch
  • Accessible materials such as gold plated, stainless steel and sterling silver

NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs, so unreleased catalogue designs stay private. Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days, which helps when a catalogue needs a late addition. International shipping to Utah is separate and confirmed once the method is known.

Outdoor and minimal brands

Like nearby Colorado, Utah's outdoor brands sell durable, simple jewelry with landscape motifs: arches, peaks and desert forms. Low-profile designs that will not snag are checked in CAD.

Workflow

Brief → NDA → CAD renders → physical sample shipped to Salt Lake City → approval → production checked against sample → shipping to your warehouse or store.

Salt Lake City is on Mountain time, eleven and a half to twelve and a half hours behind Mumbai.

Seasonal catalogue calendar

Catalogue-driven sellers live by launch dates. Map backward from each catalogue release: final designs, CAD approval, sample sign-off, production and shipping. Leave room for one round of sample revisions, since photography usually needs approved samples well before launch. Share the calendar with us at the start of the season so each design lands on time.
