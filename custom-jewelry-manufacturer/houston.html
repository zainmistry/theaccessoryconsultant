Custom jewelry manufacturer for Houston brands

Houston is one of the most diverse cities in America, and its jewelry market reflects that. Along the Harwin and Hillcroft corridors and in the Mahatma Gandhi District you will find South Asian jewelers and bridal boutiques. Galleria-area retailers serve a luxury customer. Vietnamese, Latin American, Nigerian and Middle Eastern communities each bring their own jewelry traditions, from bridal sets to gifting pieces. For a Houston buyer, a manufacturer that understands both Western fine jewelry and South Asian design is unusually useful.

The Accessory Consultant manufactures custom jewelry at our factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Houston office. Our team serves Houston clients remotely, by phone, email and video call, and ships finished pieces to your store or warehouse.

A Mumbai factory for a multicultural market

Mumbai is one of the world's major jewelry-making cities, and our team works across styles: minimal everyday gold, bridal sets, statement pieces and stone-heavy designs. For Houston retailers serving South Asian customers, that familiarity matters. You can brief us on a design tradition without explaining every detail from scratch. For retailers serving other communities, we build to your sketch, reference photos or CAD.

Remote production from Houston: what each step looks like

Talk first. Many Houston store owners prefer a call to a form. Call +91 9152727387 or email info@theaccessoryconsultant.com with photos of what you want made. Houston is on Central time, about ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai.

Agree the program. We confirm styles, quantities, material and stones. MOQ starts from 50 units per design. For private label and OEM programs, NDAs are offered before you share designs.

CAD review. Our designers build 3D models and send renders. For bridal sets, this is where you check how each element sits together.

Sample approval. A physical sample ships to you in Houston. Check weight, finish and stone setting. Your approved sample becomes the reference for production.

Production and shipping. Pieces are made and checked against that sample, then packed and shipped to Houston. Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping time to Houston is separate and is confirmed once we know the method.

Planning around wedding and festival seasons

Demand at many Houston stores peaks around wedding seasons and festivals such as Diwali, as well as the year-end holidays. Put your in-store date in the brief so we can schedule CAD and sampling with enough room. Our jewelry product development guide explains how long each stage takes in practice.
