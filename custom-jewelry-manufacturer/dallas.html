Custom jewelry manufacturer for Dallas brands

Dallas is a wholesale town. Dallas Market Center draws buyers from across the South and Midwest for gift, home and apparel markets, and accessories and jewelry are a big part of what they come to buy. Around that market sits a deep community of boutique owners, wholesale showrooms, Western and statement-jewelry brands, and fast-growing D2C labels built in Dallas and Fort Worth. The common thread: these buyers order by the line, and they want to reorder what sold.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Dallas office. Our team serves Dallas and North Texas clients remotely, and we ship production directly to your showroom, warehouse or store.

Built for market-week buyers

If you show at a market, you already think in lines and reorders. Here is how we fit that rhythm.

Before market: we develop CAD and samples for new styles so you have physical pieces to show buyers. Because MOQ starts from 50 units per design, you can sample a wide line and commit only to the styles that write orders.

After market: you send us the order totals per style. We produce against the approved samples and ship to you in Dallas. Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days for a program; for reorders of an approved style the CAD stage is already done. International shipping to Dallas is separate and confirmed once the method is known.

Between markets: reorder bestsellers against the same sample so your boutique customers get the same piece they bought last season.

What Dallas lines tend to look like

Texas taste covers a wide range. We hear requests for statement earrings and layered necklaces for boutiques, Western-inspired pieces with turquoise-look and semi-precious stones, monogram and personalised pieces, and clean gold everyday jewelry for D2C brands. We manufacture in gold plated, gold filled, gold vermeil, sterling silver, stainless steel and solid gold, with semi-precious and artificial stones among the stone options.

Private label for Dallas boutiques

Plenty of Dallas boutiques want their own label rather than a wholesale brand everyone else carries. Private label lets you sell pieces made to your design, under your name. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs, so your designs stay yours. Read our private label jewelry manufacturer guide to see how it works.

Communication across time zones

Dallas is about ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai depending on daylight saving. We work by email, phone and video call. Most Dallas clients send comments in the afternoon and have answers or updated renders the next morning.
