Custom jewelry manufacturer for San Antonio brands

San Antonio celebrates. Fiesta fills April with parades and medals, quinceañeras are a major family occasion, and the city's Tejano, Mexican-American and Western traditions all shape what people wear. Boutiques around the Pearl and Southtown, quinceañera and bridal shops across the city, and online sellers serving Texas families make up a market with a strong taste for gold, crosses, religious medals and celebration jewelry.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a San Antonio office. Our team serves San Antonio clients remotely, with sample and production shipped to your address.

Quinceañera and celebration sets

A quinceañera jewelry program often includes a tiara-coordinated necklace, earrings, a bracelet and a ring, sometimes with a religious medal. Stores need matching sets and the ability to reorder the same design season after season.

  • Materials: gold plated and gold vermeil for accessible sets; solid gold for keepsake pieces.
  • Stones: artificial stones for sparkle at accessible prices, semi-precious stones for colour.
  • Consistency: each reorder is made against the approved sample, so this year's set matches last year's.

Fiesta medals and pins

Fiesta medals are a beloved collecting tradition. Organisations, businesses and individuals design their own each year. If you are producing a medal or pin, send your artwork, size and quantity. MOQ starts from 50 units per design. Build in time for sample approval and shipping before the April season.

Religious and heritage jewelry

Crosses, Virgen de Guadalupe medals and saint pendants are steady sellers. We manufacture to your designs and approved artwork. Fine detail in small medals is checked carefully in CAD before sampling.

Western influence

San Antonio also shares Texas's Western style: silver buckles-inspired pendants, star motifs and rodeo-season pieces. Sterling silver is the usual choice.

Getting started

  1. Send designs or references, quantities and target retail price.
  2. Sign an NDA if your program is private label or OEM.
  3. Review CAD renders and request changes.
  4. Approve a physical sample shipped to San Antonio.
  5. Receive production checked against the sample.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping is separate and confirmed once the method is known. San Antonio is on Central time, ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai.
