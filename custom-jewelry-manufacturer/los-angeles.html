Custom jewelry manufacturer for Los Angeles brands

Los Angeles builds jewelry brands differently from anywhere else in the US. A large share of new labels here start online: a founder with an audience, a Shopify store, a drop schedule and a content calendar. Celebrity-led and creator-led brands, stylists who launch their own lines, and D2C companies scaling out of Venice, Silver Lake and Downtown all need a jewelry manufacturer that can keep up with launch dates set by social media rather than trade shows.

The Accessory Consultant manufactures custom and private label jewelry at our factory in Mumbai, India. We have no Los Angeles office; our team serves LA clients remotely, and most of the LA brands we speak with never need to meet in person. They need clear CAD, a sample they can photograph and a production run that looks exactly like it.

What LA brands usually ask us for

  • Drop-ready capsules. A small collection of five to fifteen styles timed to a launch. Our MOQ from 50 units per design keeps each style affordable to test.
  • Content-friendly finishes. Gold vermeil and gold filled are popular with LA D2C brands because they photograph warmly and wear well. Read our gold vermeil manufacturing guide and gold filled guide.
  • Confidential launches. Celebrity and creator brands often keep a collection under wraps until launch day. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs.
  • Fast reorders. When a piece sells out after a post, you reorder against the same approved sample.

The Downtown LA Jewelry District and where we fit

Downtown's Jewelry District around Hill Street is one of the largest in the country, and plenty of LA brands use it for casting, repairs and quick one-offs. Where a Mumbai factory helps is repeatable volume: running the same design at 50, 200 or 1,000 pieces to a single approved standard. Many founders keep a local jeweler for urgent fixes and use us as their production partner.

A typical LA timeline

Here is how a first collection usually moves, written the way most of our D2C clients plan it:

Week zero: the brief. You send mood boards, screenshots, sketches or a stylist's references. No CAD files needed. We reply with questions about materials, sizes and quantities.

CAD and revisions. Our designers model each piece and send renders. Expect comments on proportions, chain lengths and stone sizes; this is the cheapest point to change things.

The sample. A physical sample ships to you in LA. Shoot it, wear it, test it. When it is right, you approve it and it becomes the reference for production.

Production and QC. We manufacture and check every piece against the sample, then pack and ship to your LA address or your 3PL.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping time to Los Angeles is separate and is confirmed once we know where goods are going and how you want them shipped. Mumbai runs about twelve and a half to thirteen and a half hours ahead of LA, so a message sent in your evening is often answered by your morning.
