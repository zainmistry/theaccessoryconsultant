Custom jewelry manufacturer for Philadelphia brands

Jewelers' Row on Sansom Street is often described as one of the oldest diamond districts in America, and Philadelphia's jewelry identity still revolves around it. Engagement rings, wedding bands and anniversary pieces drive much of the city's trade, sold by family businesses that have served the same neighbourhoods for decades. Around them, Fishtown and South Philly have bred a newer generation of independent designers and online brands.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer based in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Philadelphia office. Our team serves Philadelphia clients remotely and ships finished production to your store or studio.

Bridal first

Philadelphia buyers ask us about bridal more than anything else, so here is how a bridal program works with us:

  1. Your design or ours. Send a ring you sell, a sketch, a CAD file or a customer request. We build or refine the CAD.
  2. Stone plan. Choose natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds or precious stones. Tell us sizes and shapes, or ask us to propose them.
  3. Metal. Most bridal runs are in solid gold; some are in sterling silver for fashion bridal lines.
  4. Sample. A physical sample ships to Philadelphia. Check setting, profile and finish.
  5. Production. Pieces are made and checked against the approved sample.

MOQ starts from 50 units per design, which suits a store that wants to stock a house mounting style rather than special-order every ring. For more, read our bridal jewelry manufacturer page and the engagement ring case study.

Keeping a family business's designs private

Long-standing stores often have signature designs that customers recognise. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs, signed before design files are shared. Your approved CAD and sample are used only for your production.

Timing

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Philadelphia is separate and is confirmed once the method is known. Philadelphia is nine and a half to ten and a half hours behind Mumbai depending on daylight saving, so most questions are answered overnight. If you are stocking for engagement season around the winter holidays and Valentine's Day, brief us well ahead.
