Custom jewelry manufacturer for Phoenix brands

Phoenix sits in the heart of the American Southwest, and its jewelry taste reflects the desert: silver, warm stones, cuffs, concho-style details and bold rings. Scottsdale's galleries and resort boutiques sell to visitors who want something that feels of the place, while Phoenix's fast-growing suburbs support D2C brands and boutique chains. Winter is high season, when snowbirds and golf and spring-training visitors fill the resorts.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Phoenix or Scottsdale office. Our team serves Arizona clients remotely.

A note on Southwestern style and authenticity

Native American jewelry is a living art form and, in the US, there are legal protections around how goods are marketed as Native American made. We do not produce or market anything as Native American made. What we can do is manufacture desert-inspired designs created by your brand, sold honestly under your brand's name. If your line draws on a specific cultural tradition, we recommend checking marketing language with your own advisers.

How a Phoenix order moves

Brief. Send sketches, photos or CAD with quantities. MOQ starts from 50 units per design. If you need an NDA for a private label or OEM program, we sign it before you share files.

CAD. We model each piece. For heavy silver designs, the render review is where you check thickness and proportion.

Sample. A physical sample ships to you in Arizona so you can feel the weight and check the stones.

Production and delivery. We manufacture against the approved sample and ship to your Phoenix or Scottsdale address.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Phoenix is separate and confirmed once the method is known.

Time zone

Most of Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so Phoenix is twelve and a half hours behind Mumbai all year. That makes scheduling simple: a call in your early morning is early evening for us.

Planning for high season

Resort and gallery stock needs to be on shelves before the winter visitors arrive. If you are planning for that season, brief us early and tell us your in-store date. Our jewelry product development guide explains how long each stage takes.
