Custom jewelry manufacturer for El Paso brands

El Paso sits on the border with Ciudad Juárez, and the two cities form one of the largest binational metro areas in the world. Retail here serves customers from both sides of the border, in English and Spanish, and jewelry taste blends Mexican, Texan and Southwestern influences: religious medals, gold chains, bold earrings, silver with desert stones. El Paso is also on Mountain time, an hour different from the rest of Texas, which matters when planning calls.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have an El Paso office. Our team serves El Paso clients remotely, in English.

What borderland retailers stock

  • Gold chains and pendants. Popular in gold plated, gold vermeil and solid gold. See our chains and necklaces page.
  • Religious jewelry. Crosses, rosary-style pieces and medals.
  • Statement earrings. Hoops and colourful designs with artificial stones.
  • Desert silver. Sterling silver with semi-precious stones.

A note on cross-border logistics

Some El Paso businesses also sell into Mexico. We ship finished goods to your US address. Any onward movement across the border, and its paperwork, is your arrangement, and we recommend speaking with a customs broker. International shipping from India to El Paso is separate from our factory timeline and is confirmed once the method is known.

How a project runs

Start with a brief: designs or photos, quantity per design, materials and target retail price. MOQ starts from 50 units per design. If the line is private label or OEM, NDAs are offered before you share files.

We build CAD and send renders. Once approved, a physical sample ships to El Paso. You check it in hand, then we produce against it and ship the order.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days.

Time zone

Because El Paso uses Mountain time, it is eleven and a half to twelve and a half hours behind Mumbai, one hour more than Dallas or Houston. Afternoon feedback from you usually gets a reply by your next morning.

Small stores, small runs

Many El Paso stores are independent and family-run. Our minimum lets them carry an own-brand range without the inventory burden of large orders. Read our wholesale guide and low MOQ guide.
