Custom jewelry manufacturer for Minneapolis brands

The Twin Cities are a retail capital. Some of America's largest retailers are headquartered in the Minneapolis area, Mall of America sits in neighbouring Bloomington, and the region has a deep pool of buyers, merchandisers and product developers who know how to run a private label program. Many of them eventually leave to start their own brands. At the same time, the North Loop and Uptown support independent boutiques with a clean, Scandinavian-influenced taste.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with its factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Minneapolis office. Our team serves Minnesota clients remotely.

For people who already speak private label

If you have worked in retail product development, you know what you want from a supplier: clear specs, an approved sample, consistent production and honest timelines. Here are ours, stated plainly:

  • MOQ: from 50 units per design.
  • Lead time: about 18 days from CAD to delivery. International shipping to Minneapolis is separate and confirmed once the method is chosen.
  • Confidentiality: NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs.
  • Standard: production is checked against the sample you approve.

We do not quote prices, certifications or plating thickness on this page. Those are confirmed per project in your quote.

Minimal Nordic taste

Minnesota customers often prefer understated jewelry: thin bands, small hoops, fine chains and simple pendants. Clean designs leave nowhere to hide, so finish and proportion matter. We check both in CAD and you approve them on the physical sample. Common materials are sterling silver, gold filled, gold vermeil and solid gold.

Winter gifting

Minnesota's long winters and holiday season concentrate jewelry sales into the final months of the year. If you are building a holiday collection, brief us early and tell us your in-store or launch date. Our jewelry product development guide helps you plan backward.

Moving from buyer to founder

If you are leaving a corporate retail role to start a brand, the difference is that you now carry the inventory risk. Low minimums let you launch a tight collection and reorder what works. Read our low MOQ guide and OEM vs ODM guide to decide how much design work you want to own.

Remote, but structured

Every Minneapolis project runs on the same documents: a written brief, CAD renders with recorded revisions, a sample, and a production standard. Minneapolis is on Central time, ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai, which gives an overnight turnaround on most questions.
