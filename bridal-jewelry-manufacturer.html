Bridal jewelry manufacturer for US brands and retailers

What a bridal program can include

Engagement rings

Solitaire, halo, three-stone, pavé band, hidden halo and vintage-inspired styles. We produce complete rings or semi-mounts, depending on how you sell. Tell us which in the brief.

Wedding bands

Plain bands, comfort-fit bands, diamond eternity and half-eternity bands, and matching bands contoured to sit against a specific engagement ring.

Metals

Bridal rings are typically made in solid gold; karat options are discussed per project. Fashion bridal and bridal party pieces may use sterling silver or gold vermeil. You approve colour and finish on the physical sample.

Why bridal CAD review matters more

In bridal, millimetres change everything. During CAD review, check:

  • Stone height. How high the centre stone sits affects both look and snagging.
  • Prong or bezel design. Number, thickness and placement of prongs.
  • Band width and thickness. Especially under the centre stone.
  • Profile view. Whether a wedding band will sit flush against the engagement ring.
  • Gallery detail. Hidden halos and side details customers discover later.

We send renders from multiple angles and revise until you approve. Read our jewelry CAD guide for more on CAD review.

Sizing and size runs

Engagement rings are often resized after purchase. Plan your size run, and discuss whether your designs (especially eternity bands) can be resized. Within the MOQ per design, tell us how to split units across sizes.
