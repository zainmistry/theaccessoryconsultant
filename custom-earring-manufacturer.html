Custom earring manufacturer for US jewelry brands

Earrings are often the first product a jewelry brand launches and the one it reorders most. They photograph well, they sell as gifts, and they do not need a size run. They are also easy to get wrong in production. A post that bends, a hinge that loosens, a hoop that closes off-center, or a pair where the two sides do not match will come back as returns long before anyone comments on the design.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom earring manufacturer for US jewelry brands, D2C labels, boutiques and wholesalers. CAD, casting and finishing happen at our factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a US office. Samples and production ship to the address you give us.

Earring styles we manufacture

Studs

Solitaire studs, stone-set shapes, plain metal shapes and logo studs. The decisions that matter are the post, the back and how the front sits against the ear. A heavy front on a short post tips forward, so we look at weight and balance in CAD before the sample.

Huggies and small hoops

Huggies are one of the most reordered everyday styles. The hinge or click closure has to open and close cleanly many times and still line up. Inner diameter, tube thickness and whether the hoop is plain, textured or stone-set are set in CAD, then checked by opening and closing the physical sample.

Hoops

Larger hoops in round, oval and shaped profiles, with hinged, latch-back or wire closures. Diameter and tube weight change how a hoop wears through a day. Write the size you want in millimeters, not only "medium," so the sample and the reorder are measured the same way.

Drops, dangles and chandeliers

Drops with a single charm, multi-tier dangles and statement pieces. Movement, length and total weight are the design questions. Jump rings and links should be sized for the weight they carry. If a drop shares a charm with a necklace in the same collection, say so in the brief so both use the same CAD.

Ear cuffs, climbers and clip styles

Cuffs and climbers follow the curve of the ear and need a careful fit check on the sample. Clip and non-pierced styles are useful for customers who do not have pierced ears. These are usually fashion pieces and are discussed per project.

Bridal and stone-set earrings

Studs, drops and halo styles for brides, bridesmaids and gifting. See our bridal jewelry manufacturer page for full bridal sets, and the lab-grown diamond jewelry manufacturer page if diamond studs and huggies are the core of your line.

Design decisions to make before CAD

Most earring problems are cheaper to fix in CAD than after casting. Settle these before you approve the model:

  1. Closure type. Post and push-back, screw-back, hinged huggie, latch-back hoop, French wire, lever-back or clip. Each changes the CAD and the feel of the pair.
  2. Size in millimeters. Hoop inner diameter, drop length and stud face size. Use numbers so the reorder is measured the same way as the sample.
  3. Weight. Heavy earrings stretch the ear and tip forward. If the design is large, ask us to look at hollow areas or a lighter profile in CAD.
  4. Pair matching. Mirror-image designs, such as cuffs and climbers, need a left and a right. Say whether the pair is identical or mirrored.
  5. Packaging count. Whether you sell pairs, singles or mixed sets changes how units are counted against the minimum. Write it down.
  6. Collection links. If an earring matches a pendant or a ring, say so. Sharing CAD across a collection keeps the pieces consistent. Our jewelry CAD manufacturing guide explains how CAD approval works.

From brief to delivery

Earring programs follow the same factory path as our other work. Nothing is skipped because a stud looks simple.

  1. Brief. Style, size in millimeters, closure, metal, finish, stones if any, and quantity per design. Sketches, reference photos or CAD files all work as a start.
  2. NDA. For private label and OEM programs, signed before files move.
  3. CAD. We model the earring and send renders. Review the front, the side profile and the closure.
  4. Sample. A physical sample ships to you. Wear it for a day. Open and close it. Check the finish and how the pair matches. That sample is the standard for the run.
  5. Casting and finishing in Mumbai. Production is cast and finished at our factory and checked against the approved sample.
  6. Delivery. Packed earrings ship to your studio or warehouse. Shipping time is quoted separately from the about-18-day CAD-to-delivery figure.
