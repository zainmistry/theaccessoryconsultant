Duties and shipping to the US

Who pays US import duty?

Import duty, brokerage, and other entry charges for a US shipment are the importer's responsibility. The amount depends on classification, customs value, and how the goods are entered. We do not quote a duty percentage. Name the destination and entry plan in the RFQ and we will keep factory timing separate from shipping.

Is shipping included in the 18 days?

About 18 days is factory CAD-to-delivery. International shipping time to the US (or other countries) is separate from factory CAD-to-delivery and is confirmed once destination and method are known.

Do you offer an NDA before CAD?

NDAs are offered for private label and OEM. Share the requirement at quote stage and we align on documentation before sensitive design files move.

Custom jewelry manufacturer and private label jewelry manufacturing for US brands

The Accessory Consultant is a jewelry manufacturer for US brands, D2C labels, and wholesale buyers. Most buyers come to us for one of three reasons: they need a private label jewelry manufacturer to produce their own designs, they want a custom jewelry manufacturer to develop a new collection, or they need a factory that works at a lower minimum than most. See the full list on our services page.

Private label, OEM, and ODM jewelry manufacturing

Private label: we manufacture your designs under your brand name. You approve the CAD and the sample, and the product ships ready for your label and packaging. This is also what many buyers call white label jewelry.

OEM: you supply the design, spec, or CAD, and we build to it exactly. Good for brands with an in-house designer and a clear technical brief.

ODM: you start from a direction rather than a finished design, and our team develops the product with you. Good for new lines and for brands that want to move faster. Read our OEM and ODM guide for how each model works.

NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs, so your designs stay confidential before they launch.

Low MOQ: start from 50 units per design

Large minimums are one of the biggest barriers for new jewelry brands. Our MOQ starts from 50 units per design. That lets a D2C startup test a small capsule, lets a wholesale buyer trial a new style, and lets an established brand add a design without tying up cash in stock it may not sell. If a design works, you reorder against the same approved sample. Our low MOQ jewelry manufacturer guide explains how to plan a first order.

From CAD to delivery in about 18 days

Every program follows the same path, so you always know what comes next:

  1. Consultation: share your designs, references, quantities, and timeline.
  2. CAD: we build a 3D model and you review renders before any metal is used.
  3. Sample: a physical piece for you to approve. It becomes the reference for production.
  4. Production: pieces are made and checked against the approved sample.
  5. Delivery: finished goods are packed and shipped to your destination.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping time to the US is separate and is confirmed once the destination and method are known. New to this? Our jewelry product development guide covers each stage in detail, and the process page shows how we run it.
