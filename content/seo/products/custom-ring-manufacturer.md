# Custom ring manufacturer for US brands

Rings are the hardest everyday jewelry to get right. They take more wear than any other piece, they have to fit a finger precisely, and customers notice small differences in profile, comfort and finish. A ring that looks perfect in a photo can feel wrong on the hand. That is why choosing a custom ring manufacturer is less about who can cast metal and more about who can control proportion, sizing and consistency across a production run.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with its own factory in Mumbai, India. We make rings for US brands, D2C labels and wholesalers, working remotely with clients across the country. We do not have a US office.

## Ring types we manufacture

### Stacking and everyday bands
Thin bands, textured bands and stackable sets are a staple of D2C collections. They look simple, but their thinness leaves no room for error: an uneven band or soft edge is immediately visible. We check width and thickness in CAD and you confirm them on the physical sample.

### Signet and statement rings
Signets, chunky domes and men's rings carry weight and surface area. Face size, shank thickness and the transition between them define the look. Engraving-ready faces can be built into the design.

### Stone-set fashion rings
Solitaire-style, halo-style and cluster designs set with [semi-precious stones](/stones/semi-precious-stones), [artificial stones](/stones/artificial-stones) or [precious stones](/stones/precious-stones). Setting style affects both look and durability, and we discuss it at the CAD stage.

### Engagement rings and wedding bands
For bridal programs we work in [solid gold](/jewelry/solid-gold) with [natural diamonds](/stones/natural-diamonds) or [lab-grown diamonds](/stones/lab-grown-diamonds). See our dedicated [bridal jewelry manufacturer](/bridal-jewelry-manufacturer) page and the [engagement ring case study](/case-studies/1).

## Sizing: plan it before CAD

Sizing is the most common source of problems in ring programs. Decide early:

1. **Which US sizes you will offer.** Most brands offer a core range rather than every size.
2. **How you will split your quantity.** MOQ is per design, so you need to plan how many units each size gets within your order. Tell us your split in the brief.
3. **Whether the design scales.** Patterns and stone layouts that look right at one size may need adjustment at another. We check this in CAD.
4. **Comfort fit or flat interior.** Interior profile changes how a ring feels and can affect sizing perception.

Our [RFQ guide](/blogs/how-to-get-a-jewelry-manufacturing-quote-rfq-guide-us) includes a checklist for size-run planning.

## The ring development process

**Brief.** Send sketches, reference photos, an existing ring you own the rights to, or CAD files. Include size range, material, stones and quantity per design.

**NDA.** For private label and OEM programs, signed before files are shared.

**CAD.** We build a 3D model and renders. Ring CAD review should focus on width, thickness, stone height and profile from the side, which photos often hide. Our [jewelry CAD guide](/blogs/jewelry-cad-manufacturing-for-brand-development) explains what to look for.

**Sample.** A physical sample ships to your US address. Try it on. Check comfort, weight and finish.

**Production.** Your rings are made and checked against the approved sample.

**Delivery.** Packed goods ship to your studio, warehouse or 3PL.
