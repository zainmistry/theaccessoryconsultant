# Wholesale wedding band manufacturer for US jewelers

A wedding band is a simple object with very little room for error. The profile has to be comfortable every day, the size has to be the size you ordered, and a matching pair has to look like a pair. For a jeweler or retailer ordering in volume, that consistency matters more than a one-off bench piece. You need the same band, in a planned size run, finished the same way on every unit.

The Accessory Consultant is a wholesale wedding band manufacturer for US jewelers, bridal retailers and wholesalers. The work is done at our factory in Mumbai, India: CAD, casting and finishing. We do not have a US office. Samples and production ship to the address you give us.

Three terms apply to every band program:

- **MOQ from 50 units per design.** A plain band and a diamond band are different designs. Within one design, you split the 50 units across the finger sizes you actually stock.
- **About 18 days from CAD to delivery** for a typical program. International shipping to the US is separate and is confirmed once the destination and method are known.
- **NDAs offered for private label and OEM** programs, signed before design files are shared.

## Band types we manufacture in volume

### Plain and comfort-fit bands
Flat, half-round, knife-edge and comfort-fit interiors. Comfort fit changes how a band feels and how it sits next to another ring. Width and thickness are decided in CAD and confirmed on the physical sample, because a 1mm difference is obvious on the hand.

### Diamond and eternity bands
Half-eternity, full eternity and channel or pavé bands. Stone size, spacing and how the band can be sized later should be decided before CAD is approved. Eternity bands are often not resized the way a plain band is. Tell us which sizes you will stock so the run matches the tray.

### Bands matched to an engagement ring
A band that must sit flush against a specific engagement ring needs the engagement ring's profile, not a guess. Send the ring, a photo with measurements, or the CAD. We check the fit in CAD and you confirm it on the sample. See our [custom ring manufacturer](/custom-ring-manufacturer) page for signets, stacking bands and engagement rings, and our [bridal jewelry manufacturer](/bridal-jewelry-manufacturer) page for sets.

### His and hers pairs
Two profiles designed as a pair. They can share a metal and finish or be specified separately. Each profile is its own design for the minimum.

## How to plan a size run inside the minimum

The minimum is per design, not per size. A jeweler who wants one comfort-fit band in eight sizes puts the quantity split in the brief: how many size 6, size 7, size 8, and so on, adding up to at least 50 for that design.

Decide this before CAD:

1. **Which US sizes you will stock.** Most stores carry a core range and reorder the sizes that sell, rather than every half size on day one.
2. **The split.** Write the units per size. We manufacture that split.
3. **Whether the profile changes with size.** A very wide band or a stone layout may need a check at the small and large ends. We look at that in CAD.
4. **Comfort fit or flat interior.** This is a design decision, not a note to add after casting.

## From CAD through casting and finishing

Wholesale bands follow the same factory path as our other programs. Nothing is skipped because the shape looks simple.

1. **Brief.** Profile, width, thickness, metal, stones if any, size split, and quantity. Sketches, a band you already sell and have the right to reproduce, or CAD files all work as a start.
2. **NDA.** For private label and OEM house bands, signed before files move.
3. **CAD.** We model the band and send renders. Review width, height, interior shape and, if it must match an engagement ring, the side profile.
4. **Sample.** A physical sample ships to your store. Wear it. Check edges, weight and finish. That sample is the standard for the run.
5. **Casting and finishing in Mumbai.** Production is cast and finished at our factory and checked against the approved sample.
6. **Delivery.** Packed bands ship to your store, studio or warehouse. Shipping time is quoted separately from the about-18-day CAD-to-delivery figure.

## Private label house bands

Many jewelers want the band under their own name, not a supplier's catalog number. That is a private label program: your profile, your metal callout, your reorder. We manufacture to the CAD and sample you approve. We do not sell a public catalog of bands on this site.

If the design is yours, say so. If you need help turning a bench sketch into a production model, say that too. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM so a signature profile is not shared as someone else's sample.

Reorders use the approved CAD and the approved sample. Write down the width, metal and finish you actually approved, not the first mood board. That is what keeps the second tray matching the first.
