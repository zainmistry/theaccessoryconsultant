# Custom jewelry manufacturer for US brands

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer and private label jewelry manufacturer working with brands, D2C labels and wholesale buyers across the United States. Production happens at our own factory in Mumbai, India. We do not run a US office. Our team serves US clients remotely, by email, phone and video call, and ships finished goods to your warehouse, studio or fulfilment partner.

If you have been searching for a "jewelry manufacturer" that will take a small first order, protect your designs and move quickly, these are the three facts that matter most:

- **MOQ from 50 units per design**, so you can launch or test a style without a large stock commitment.
- **About 18 days from CAD to delivery** for a typical program. International shipping time to the US is separate and is confirmed once the destination and method are known.
- **NDAs offered for private label and OEM** programs, signed before you share design files.

## Who we manufacture for in the US

US buyers usually fall into one of four groups. **Independent and D2C brands** selling through Shopify, Instagram and marketplaces want low minimums, consistent finish and fast reorders. **Fashion and lifestyle labels** add jewelry to apparel or accessories ranges and need pieces that match a seasonal look. **Wholesalers and retail buyers** need repeatable quality across larger runs. **Designers and bridal studios** want custom pieces built exactly to a sketch or CAD file.

## How working with a Mumbai factory works

1. **Brief:** send sketches, reference photos, a CAD file or a written idea, plus target quantity and material.
2. **NDA:** for private label and OEM programs we sign an NDA before design files change hands.
3. **CAD:** we build a 3D model and send renders for your review and revisions.
4. **Sample:** a physical sample is made and sent for your approval. It becomes the production reference.
5. **Production:** pieces are made and checked against the approved sample.
6. **Shipping:** finished goods are packed and shipped to your US address.
