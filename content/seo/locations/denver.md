# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Denver brands

Colorado brands sell to people who hike, climb and ski, and their jewelry follows. Denver's outdoor and lifestyle companies, the gift shops of mountain towns from Boulder to Aspen, and a busy scene of independent designers in RiNo and South Broadway all ask for pieces that are simple, durable and meaningful: mountain outlines, coordinates, trail markers and minimal everyday metal.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We have no Denver office. Our team serves Colorado clients remotely.

## Durable by design

Outdoor customers are tough on jewelry. When we work with Colorado brands, three design questions come up early:

1. **Metal.** [Stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel) and [sterling silver](/jewelry/silver) are common choices for active wear; [gold filled](/jewelry/gold-filled) suits everyday pieces. We explain options and you approve finish on a physical sample.
2. **Profile.** Low-profile settings and smooth edges are less likely to snag on gear. We check this in CAD.
3. **Clasps and chain.** Secure closures matter for necklaces and bracelets worn outdoors. Raise it in the brief and we will build it into the design.

## Process and timing

- **Brief:** sketches, photos, coordinates or artwork, with quantities.
- **NDA:** for private label and OEM programs, before files are shared.
- **CAD:** 3D models and renders for your review.
- **Sample:** a physical piece shipped to Denver.
- **Production:** manufactured and checked against the approved sample.
- **Delivery:** packed and shipped to your Colorado address.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Denver is separate and confirmed once the method is known. Denver is on Mountain time, eleven and a half to twelve and a half hours behind Mumbai.

## Seasonality

Colorado retail has two peaks: summer trail season and winter ski season. Put your on-shelf date in the brief. Our [jewelry product development guide](/blogs/jewelry-product-development) shows how to plan stages backward from a launch.
