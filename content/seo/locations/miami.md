# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Miami brands

Miami jewelry sells in the sun. The brands we hear from in Miami are often tied to resort wear, swimwear, boutique hotels and beach lifestyle, and their customers want pieces they can wear from the pool to dinner. Add Miami's role as a bridge to Latin American buyers, the energy of Art Basel week every December, and the boutiques of the Design District and Wynwood, and you get a market that wants colour, stacking pieces and fast seasonal turnover.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer based in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Miami office. Our team serves Miami clients remotely and ships finished production to your Florida address.

## Designing for a warm-weather customer

Miami collections raise practical questions that colder markets rarely ask. Will this piece be worn at the beach? In chlorinated pools? Layered with five other chains? These questions shape material choice more than anything else:

- **Stainless steel** is a common choice for swim and active lines. See our [stainless steel jewelry](/jewelry/stainless-steel) page.
- **Gold filled** suits everyday stacking pieces. Our [gold filled manufacturing guide](/blogs/gold-filled-manufacturer-for-us-d2c-brands) explains the trade-offs.
- **Gold vermeil and sterling silver** work for elevated resort pieces sold in boutiques.
- **Semi-precious and coloured stones** bring the colour Miami buyers ask for. Browse our [stone options](/stones).

We will tell you what each option is suited to on the sample, and you approve the finish on a physical piece before production starts.

## Season planning, Miami style

Miami has its own calendar. Winter is peak tourist and event season, and Swim Week arrives in early summer. If you are building around either, tell us your in-store or launch date in the brief.

Our process does not change by city, but the order you care about does. For most Miami brands the critical point is the sample, because that is what you shoot for the season's campaign:

1. You send references, sketches or a CAD file. We sign an NDA first for private label and OEM programs.
2. Our team builds CAD and sends renders for comments.
3. A sample ships to you in Miami. You check colour, weight and wear.
4. Once approved, we manufacture and check each piece against that sample.
5. Goods are packed and shipped to Miami.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Miami is separate and is confirmed once we know the method. Mumbai is about nine and a half to ten and a half hours ahead of Miami, which gives a near-overnight turnaround on CAD comments.
