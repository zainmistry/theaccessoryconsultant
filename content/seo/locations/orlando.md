# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Orlando brands

Orlando welcomes tens of millions of visitors a year, and a large share of the city's retail is built around them: theme-park-area gift shops, resort boutiques, International Drive retail and convention-centre vendors. That creates steady demand for souvenir and keepsake jewelry, character-free themed pieces, charms and event merchandise, alongside a growing community of local D2C brands.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have an Orlando office. Our team serves Central Florida clients remotely.

## Souvenir jewelry that people actually wear

The best-selling keepsakes are simple: a charm, a pendant, a bracelet with a place name or motif. To keep them affordable, many tourism retailers choose [gold plated](/jewelry/gold-plated) or [stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel), with [artificial stones](/stones/artificial-stones) for colour. Premium resort boutiques may prefer [sterling silver](/jewelry/silver) or [gold vermeil](/jewelry/gold-vermeil).

A note on licensing: if a design uses a trademarked character, park name or logo, you need the rights holder's permission. We manufacture your own original designs or designs you are licensed to use.

## Event and convention merchandise

Orlando's convention calendar is busy. Associations and companies running events sometimes want jewelry as a gift or as event merchandise: lapel pins, charm bracelets, pendants with event branding. Give us the event date in the brief. MOQ starts from 50 units per design.

## Keeping shelves full through the year

Tourism retail is high-volume and repeat-driven. Once a design sells, you want the next batch to look identical. Every reorder is made against your approved sample, so a charm you first stocked last spring matches the one you reorder in autumn.
