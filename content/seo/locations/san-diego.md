# Custom jewelry manufacturer for San Diego brands

San Diego jewelry is made to be worn outside. Surf and skate labels in Encinitas and Pacific Beach, beach-lifestyle boutiques in La Jolla and Coronado, and a busy community of online brands share one customer: someone who wears their jewelry in salt water, sun and sand and does not want to take it off. Add the city's large military community, its craft-brewery and wedding-tourism economy, and its proximity to Mexico, and San Diego has a very particular demand profile.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a San Diego office. Our team serves Southern California clients remotely.

## Questions San Diego founders ask first

**"Will it survive the ocean?"** This depends on material and design. [Stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel) is a common choice for surf and active lines; [sterling silver](/jewelry/silver) and [gold filled](/jewelry/gold-filled) suit beach-lifestyle pieces. Tell us how the piece will be worn and we will talk through options. You approve finish on the physical sample before any production.

**"Can I start small?"** Yes. MOQ starts from 50 units per design, so you can test shell, wave or charm designs before committing to deep stock.

**"Can I keep my designs private?"** NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs.

**"How fast?"** Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to San Diego is separate and confirmed once the method is known.

## Common San Diego product types

- Anklets, chains and layering necklaces for beachwear

- Engagement and wedding pieces for the city's destination-wedding market, in [solid gold](/jewelry/solid-gold) with [natural](/stones/natural-diamonds) or [lab-grown diamonds](/stones/lab-grown-diamonds)

## The working rhythm

San Diego is on Pacific time, twelve and a half to thirteen and a half hours behind Mumbai. Practically, you work your day, we work ours, and each side wakes up to progress.

A first collection typically goes: brief → NDA if needed → CAD renders and revisions → physical sample to San Diego → approval → production checked against the sample → packed and shipped to your studio or 3PL.
