# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Fort Worth brands

Fort Worth calls itself the city "where the West begins," and its retail lives up to it. The Stockyards, rodeo season and a thriving ranch-lifestyle economy support Western wear stores, boot and hat shops, and boutiques selling turquoise-coloured stones, silver conchos, cross pendants and buckles-inspired jewelry. Many of these businesses are family-owned and want their own house line rather than another brand's.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Fort Worth office. Our team serves Fort Worth and Tarrant County clients remotely.

## Western jewelry, made to your design

Western style is detail-heavy: rope edges, engraved scrollwork, stamped stars, longhorn and horseshoe motifs. Fine detail is checked closely in CAD, and you confirm depth and sharpness on a physical sample before production.

Typical choices:

- [Sterling silver](/jewelry/silver) for traditional Western pieces
- [Gold plated](/jewelry/gold-plated) and [stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel) for accessible boutique lines
- [Semi-precious stones](/stones/semi-precious-stones) and [artificial stones](/stones/artificial-stones) in desert and turquoise tones

## Rodeo, livestock-show and award pieces

Rodeo season and livestock shows create demand for award and commemorative jewelry: event pendants, belt-buckle-inspired designs and sponsor gifts. Send the artwork, quantity and event date. MOQ starts from 50 units per design. Make sure you hold rights to any event logo.

## Western boutiques building a house brand

A private label line gives a Western store something no competitor can stock. We start with your sketches or favourite in-store styles you own the rights to, build CAD, send a sample and produce. Every reorder matches the approved sample. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs. Our [private label guide](/blogs/private-label-jewelry-manufacturer-for-us-brands) covers the full process.

## Fort Worth versus Dallas buyers

Fort Worth and Dallas share a metroplex but often buy differently. Dallas briefs lean toward fashion and luxury retail; Fort Worth briefs are more often Western, family-run and event-driven. Our [Dallas page](/custom-jewelry-manufacturer/dallas) covers the other side of the metroplex.
