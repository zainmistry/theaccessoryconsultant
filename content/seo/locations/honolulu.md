# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Honolulu brands

Hawaii has its own jewelry language. Hawaiian heirloom bracelets with engraved names, plumeria and honu motifs, fishhook pendants and pieces with ocean themes are part of everyday island life and an important part of what visitors take home. Honolulu's Waikiki resort boutiques, Ala Moana retailers and local island-lifestyle brands sell to both residents and tourists.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Hawaii office. Our team serves Honolulu clients remotely.

## Respecting island traditions

Many Hawaiian designs carry cultural meaning. If your line draws on Native Hawaiian motifs, we recommend designing with care and checking marketing language with your own advisers. We manufacture your brand's designs and do not market them as made in Hawaii. Pieces we produce are made in India.

## Designing for salt, sun and swimming

Island customers wear jewelry in the ocean. Tell us how a piece will be worn and we will discuss materials: [stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel) for active wear, [sterling silver](/jewelry/silver) and [gold filled](/jewelry/gold-filled) for everyday pieces, and [solid gold](/jewelry/solid-gold) for heirloom and keepsake items. You approve finish on a physical sample.

## Popular Honolulu programs

- **Heirloom-style bangles and bracelets** with space for engraving
- **Resort boutique private label** pendants and earrings sold under the hotel or shop brand
- **Wedding and vow-renewal pieces** for the destination-wedding market, with [lab-grown](/stones/lab-grown-diamonds) or [natural diamonds](/stones/natural-diamonds)
- **Ocean-motif charms** for gift shops

MOQ starts from 50 units per design, which lets a resort shop carry a range of motifs without overstocking.

## Distance and planning

Honolulu is far from most suppliers, including us. Hawaii does not observe daylight saving time, so Honolulu is fifteen and a half hours behind Mumbai all year. In practice, your afternoon is our early morning of the next day, which works well for quick replies.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Hawaii is separate and confirmed once the method is known; allow for it when planning launches around peak visitor seasons.

## What to send

Designs or references, quantities, materials, engraving needs, delivery address and launch date. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs. Our [RFQ guide](/blogs/how-to-get-a-jewelry-manufacturing-quote-rfq-guide-us) shows a complete request.

## Packaging for gift buyers

Visitors buying jewelry as a keepsake often want it ready to give. If your boutique sells to travellers, plan packaging with the product: a card explaining a motif's meaning, a pouch that fits a carry-on, a tag with the island name. Mention packaging in your brief so it is planned alongside the sample rather than added at the last minute.
