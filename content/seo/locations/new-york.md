# Custom jewelry manufacturer for New York brands

New York is the densest jewelry market in the country. The Diamond District on West 47th Street concentrates dealers, setters and wholesale showrooms on a single block, while the Garment District and SoHo are home to fashion labels that treat jewelry as part of every collection. That mix gives New York buyers a very specific set of expectations: they know what good work looks like, they move to fashion-calendar deadlines, and they compare every supplier against the workshops they can walk into downtown.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with its factory in Mumbai. We do not have a New York office. Our team serves New York clients remotely, and many of the brands we talk to use us alongside, not instead of, their local network: local setters for one-off repairs and showroom pieces, our factory for repeatable production runs.

## The New York buyers we hear from most

**Fashion and ready-to-wear labels.** Designers showing during New York Fashion Week often want jewelry that ties into a seasonal story. They need samples fast enough to style a lookbook and then a production run that matches the sample. Our MOQ from 50 units per design suits capsule drops that sit beside an apparel range.

**Fine and demi-fine D2C brands.** Brooklyn and Manhattan have produced many direct-to-consumer brands selling gold vermeil, gold filled and solid gold everyday pieces online. These brands care about consistent finish from order to order, because their reviews depend on it.

**Diamond District wholesalers and dealers.** Some buyers on 47th Street source stones locally and need settings, mountings or finished lines produced at volume. We work with [natural diamonds](/stones/natural-diamonds) and [lab-grown diamonds](/stones/lab-grown-diamonds) and can quote a program around your stone direction.

**Bridal studios.** Engagement and wedding bands are a large share of New York demand. Our [luxury engagement ring case study](/case-studies/1) shows how a bridal program runs with us.

## Why a New York label would brief a Mumbai factory

Manhattan workshop space is expensive, and small-batch production in the city is often priced for one-off pieces rather than runs of 50 or 500. A dedicated export factory lets a New York brand keep design and sales in the city while production happens at a scale built for repeat orders. The trade-off is distance, and the way we handle distance is with a fixed, visible process.

## How a New York program runs, step by step

| Stage | What happens | What you approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | You send sketches, mood boards, CAD or a tech pack | Scope and quantities |
| NDA | Signed for private label and OEM before files are shared | Confidentiality terms |
| CAD | We model each piece in 3D and send renders | Proportions and details |
| Sample | A physical sample ships to your New York studio | Finish, weight, fit |
| Production | Pieces are made and checked against your sample | Final release |
| Delivery | Goods are packed and shipped to your NYC address | Receipt |

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to New York is separate and is confirmed once we know your address and preferred method. Time zones help here: Mumbai is roughly nine and a half to ten and a half hours ahead of New York, so revisions you send at the end of your day are often worked on before you are back at your desk.
