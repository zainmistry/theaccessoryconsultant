# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Charlotte brands

Charlotte is one of America's largest banking centres, and that shapes its jewelry demand in a way visitors might not expect: corporate gifting, employee milestones and client appreciation programs are a real market. Alongside, the Carolinas have a strong Southern boutique culture built on monograms, initials, pearls-and-gold classics and gifts for weddings, graduations and sorority events. SouthPark and Dilworth boutiques and a wave of online sellers serve that customer.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Charlotte office. Our team serves Charlotte and Carolinas clients remotely.

## Corporate and milestone jewelry

Companies buy jewelry for service anniversaries, recognition awards and client gifts. These programs need consistency across every unit and a clean approval process.

**What we need from you:** the logo or design, quantity per design, material, any engraving requirement, the delivery date and who signs off.

**What you get:** CAD renders for internal review, a physical sample shipped to Charlotte, and production checked against that sample. MOQ starts from 50 units per design, which suits annual award programs.

Typical materials for corporate pieces are [sterling silver](/jewelry/silver), [gold vermeil](/jewelry/gold-vermeil), [solid gold](/jewelry/solid-gold) for senior awards, and [stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel) for cufflinks and accessories.

## Monogram and Southern boutique lines

Initial and monogram jewelry is a Carolinas staple. Boutiques often want a house range of initial pendants, monogram rings and personalised pieces. We can produce the base designs in your chosen material; tell us how personalisation will be handled so we design accordingly. Our [private label jewelry manufacturer guide](/blogs/private-label-jewelry-manufacturer-for-us-brands) explains house-brand programs.

## Wedding and bridal party gifts

The Southern wedding market buys bridesmaid jewelry, mother-of-the-bride gifts and groomsmen cufflinks. These are often matching sets in modest quantities, a good fit for our minimum. For engagement and wedding bands, see our [bridal jewelry manufacturer page](/bridal-jewelry-manufacturer).

## Confidentiality and timing

NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs. Corporate clients may also prefer that their logo files are used only for their order, which is our practice.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Charlotte is separate and confirmed once the method is known. Charlotte is on Eastern time, nine and a half to ten and a half hours behind Mumbai.
