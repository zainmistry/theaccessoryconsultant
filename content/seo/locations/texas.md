# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Texas brands

Texas is not one jewelry market but several. Dallas leans toward fashion and luxury retail. Houston's diverse population and energy economy support fine jewelry and cultural pieces. Austin is a startup factory for D2C labels. San Antonio celebrates with Fiesta medals and quinceañera sets. Fort Worth is unmistakably Western. El Paso and the Rio Grande Valley are borderland markets with strong gold traditions. A brand that sells statewide often needs to speak to more than one of them.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Texas office. Our team serves clients across Texas remotely.

## The same terms statewide

Whether you are in Lubbock or Galveston, the commercial terms are identical:

- MOQ from 50 units per design
- About 18 days from CAD to delivery for a typical program
- NDAs offered for private label and OEM programs
- Production checked against the sample you approve

International shipping to Texas is separate from the factory timeline and is confirmed once the method is known.

## Time zones in Texas

Most of Texas is on Central time, ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai. El Paso and the far west of the state use Mountain time, one hour further behind. Overnight turnaround on questions works across both.

## Statewide brands

A Texas lifestyle brand with stores in several cities can run one private label program with city-specific designs: a Western range for Fort Worth stores, a colourful range for Austin, gold pieces for the Valley.
