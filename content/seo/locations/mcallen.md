# Custom jewelry manufacturer for McAllen and the Rio Grande Valley

The Rio Grande Valley, stretching from McAllen and Edinburg to Harlingen and Brownsville, is a retail region with a strong cross-border shopping tradition. McAllen's malls and stores have long drawn shoppers from northern Mexico, and the Valley's own fast-growing, family-oriented population supports jewelry stores, quinceañera and bridal boutiques, flea markets and online sellers. Gold is central to local taste.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have an office in the Valley. Our team serves Rio Grande Valley clients remotely.

## Gold-forward retail

Valley jewelry stores sell a lot of gold: chains, nameplates, hoops, pendants and rings. We produce to your designs in [solid gold](/jewelry/solid-gold), with karat options discussed per project, and in [gold vermeil](/jewelry/gold-vermeil) and [gold plated](/jewelry/gold-plated) for lower price points. Tell us your target retail price and we will help match material to it.

## Family occasions

Baptisms, first communions, quinceañeras and weddings all come with jewelry. Stores that stock occasion sets benefit from matching, reorderable designs:

- Baptism and communion medals and crosses
- Quinceañera sets with [artificial stones](/stones/artificial-stones) or [semi-precious stones](/stones/semi-precious-stones)
- Wedding bands and engagement rings with [natural](/stones/natural-diamonds) or [lab-grown diamonds](/stones/lab-grown-diamonds)

Each reorder is made against the approved sample. See our [bridal jewelry manufacturer page](/bridal-jewelry-manufacturer).

## Wholesale and flea-market sellers

Many Valley sellers buy in volume for resale. MOQ starts from 50 units per design, so a seller can carry a broad assortment of styles. Our [wholesale guide](/blogs/wholesale-jewelry-manufacturer-for-us-retailers) explains how to plan an assortment.

## Logistics

We ship finished goods to your US address in the Valley. If you resell across the border, the onward shipment and its paperwork are your arrangement; a customs broker can advise. International shipping from India is separate from our factory timeline and is confirmed once the method is known.

## Step by step

- Send your designs, quantities, materials and target price.
- NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs.
- Review CAD renders.
- Approve a physical sample shipped to you.
- Receive production checked against the sample, typically about 18 days from CAD to delivery.

McAllen is on Central time, ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai.

## Nameplates and personalised gold

Nameplate necklaces and initial rings are Valley favourites, often bought for birthdays and graduations. If personalisation is central to your store, explain your model: a stocked alphabet of initials can run as separate designs at our minimum, while one-off names follow a different process. We will help you pick the approach that fits your volumes and counter space.
