# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Chicago brands

Chicago buyers tend to be practical. The city has long been a centre for wholesale, merchandising and retail headquarters, and the Merchandise Mart remains a landmark for showrooms and buyers. Jewelers Row on Wabash Avenue has served bridal and fine-jewelry customers for generations. The Chicago brands and buyers who contact us usually want three things in writing: the minimum, the timeline and how quality will be checked.

The Accessory Consultant is a jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Chicago office. Our team serves Chicago and Midwest clients remotely and ships production to your address.

## The short answers Chicago buyers ask for

- **Minimum:** MOQ from 50 units per design.
- **Timeline:** about 18 days from CAD to delivery for a typical program. International shipping to Chicago is separate and confirmed once the method is known.
- **Confidentiality:** NDAs offered for private label and OEM programs.
- **Quality reference:** every production piece is checked against a physical sample you have approved.

## Chicago buyer types

### Regional retailers and department-store buyers
Retail buyers headquartered in Chicago often need a private label range that can sit across many stores. What matters is repeatability: the hundredth piece has to match the first. ## How remote production works from Chicago

Chicago is about ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai. In practice, most clients send comments at the end of their day and see updated CAD or answers the next morning.

**Step one, quote.** Send what you have: sketches, photos, a spec sheet or CAD. Include quantities per design, material and the delivery address. Our [RFQ guide](/blogs/how-to-get-a-jewelry-manufacturing-quote-rfq-guide-us) lists what to include.

**Step two, NDA and CAD.** For private label and OEM work we sign an NDA before design files are shared, then build 3D models for review.

**Step three, sample.** A physical sample ships to you in Chicago. Check it against your spec: weight, finish, clasp, stone setting.

**Step four, production and QC.** Pieces are made and checked against the approved sample.

**Step five, shipping.** Finished goods are packed and shipped to your Chicago warehouse, store or distribution centre.

## Why not just source domestically?

Many Chicago buyers do both. Domestic workshops are useful for repairs and rush one-offs. A dedicated export factory is built for running the same design at volume to one standard, with a low starting minimum. If you are comparing, ask every supplier the same questions: MOQ, CAD-to-delivery time, how samples are approved and whether they sign an NDA.
