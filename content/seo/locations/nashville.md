# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Nashville brands

Music City sells a look. Broadway's honky-tonks, the boutiques of 12 South and The Gulch, and a steady stream of visitors on weekend trips have made Nashville a strong market for Western-influenced and statement jewelry. On top of that, the music industry itself buys jewelry: tour merchandise, artist-branded collections and gifts for crews and teams.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with its factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Nashville office. Our team serves Tennessee clients remotely.

## Three Nashville programs

### Artist and tour merchandise
Jewelry is a higher-value alternative to standard merch. A tour or artist line might include a logo pendant, a lyric bar necklace or a signature ring. Merch programs often have hard dates tied to a tour launch, so put the date in your brief. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs, useful when a design ties to an unannounced album or tour. Confirm you hold the rights to any artwork, logo or lyrics you send.

### Boutique private label
Nashville boutiques sell to visitors and locals who want something with a bit of attitude: horseshoe, boot and star motifs, layered necklaces, turquoise-coloured stones. A private label range lets the store sell these under its own name. MOQ starts from 50 units per design.

### Bachelorette and group gifting
Group-trip shoppers buy matching pieces. Charm bracelets, initial necklaces and simple rings made in [gold plated](/jewelry/gold-plated) or [stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel) keep prices accessible for this market.

## Materials and stones

For Western statement pieces, [sterling silver](/jewelry/silver) is the classic choice. [Gold vermeil](/jewelry/gold-vermeil) and [gold filled](/jewelry/gold-filled) suit everyday boutique lines. For colour, choose [semi-precious stones](/stones/semi-precious-stones) or [artificial stones](/stones/artificial-stones). We talk through options and you approve finish on the physical sample.

## How production works

We take your brief and NDA, build CAD and share renders, then ship a physical sample to Nashville. Once you approve it, we produce against that sample and ship packed goods to your store, office or merch fulfilment partner. Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Nashville is separate and confirmed once the method is known.

Nashville is on Central time, ten and a half to eleven and a half hours behind Mumbai.

## Before you brief us

Gather: artwork or sketches, quantities per design, preferred materials, target retail price, delivery address and any deadline. Our [RFQ guide](/blogs/how-to-get-a-jewelry-manufacturing-quote-rfq-guide-us) shows what a complete request looks like.
