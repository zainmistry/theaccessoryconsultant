# Custom jewelry manufacturer for Detroit brands

Detroit knows manufacturing better than almost any city in America, and its newer consumer brands lean into that heritage. Corktown, Midtown and Eastern Market are home to labels that sell durable, industrial-looking goods with a strong sense of place, and Detroit pride is a product category in its own right. Jewelry fits: heavy rings, chains, cuffs and pendants that reference the city's grit and design history.

The Accessory Consultant is a custom jewelry manufacturer with a factory in Mumbai, India. We do not have a Detroit office. Our team serves Michigan clients remotely.

## Being honest about origin

Detroit brands care about where things are made, and so should their marketing. Pieces we manufacture are made in India. If your brand designs in Detroit and manufactures with us, say so accurately; it is a perfectly good story about design. We recommend checking country-of-origin wording with your own advisers.

## Men's jewelry and industrial design

Many Detroit brands sell to men or sell unisex. Popular requests:

- **Signet and heavy band rings** in [sterling silver](/jewelry/silver), [stainless steel](/jewelry/stainless-steel) or [solid gold](/jewelry/solid-gold)
- **Chains** with substantial links, in silver or steel
- **Pendants** with mechanical, automotive-inspired or architectural motifs
- **Cufflinks and accessories** for formal wear

Weight and proportion are critical for men's pieces. We review both in CAD and you confirm on the physical sample. Our [custom rings](/custom-ring-manufacturer) and [chains and necklaces](/chain-necklace-manufacturer) pages go deeper.

## How the work flows

**First:** a brief with artwork, sketches or reference photos, quantities and material. MOQ starts from 50 units per design. NDAs are offered for private label and OEM programs.

**Then:** CAD models and renders, revised until you approve.

**Next:** a physical sample shipped to Detroit.

**Finally:** production against the approved sample, packed and shipped to your shop, studio or warehouse.

Typical CAD-to-delivery is about 18 days. International shipping to Detroit is separate and confirmed once the method is known. Detroit is on Eastern time, nine and a half to ten and a half hours behind Mumbai.

## Apparel and lifestyle brands adding jewelry

City-pride apparel brands often add jewelry as a higher-margin category. Since you already have customers and a look, the job is to translate graphics into metal. Line artwork translates well into pendants and rings; complex gradients do not. We will tell you what works in CAD.
